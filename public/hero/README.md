# Hero assets

The official logo is `../mma-logo.png`, unchanged. These assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the user's supplied coffee-glass image, not from the outdated project notes. WebP conversion preserves alpha and caps source width at 800 px; Next.js supplies responsive sizes.

- `coffee-front.webp`: front cutout, source `ChatGPT Image 17 Oct 2026, 18_35_30.png`.
- `glass-empty.webp`: frame-locked empty variant of that front cutout.
- `glass-milk.webp`: frame-locked milk-only variant of that front cutout.
- `coffee-angle.webp`: elevated photographic view of the same type of glass. The closer detail is a gentle crop/scale of this photograph; no fake 3D orbit.

## Generation prompts (final asset specification)

Front: Extract only the exact traditional Indian grooved glass and its coffee-and-milk contents. Preserve the slender tapered silhouette and liquid marbling. Remove all background, steam, floor, shadows, glow and decorative lighting. Full upright front view, near-level camera, thick clear base and long flutes. Calm level below the rim. Portrait 2:3, centered, full glass visible. Genuine transparent alpha, soft neutral light for an ivory website, no text, logo, props, pouring or splashes.

Empty edit: Keep exact glass silhouette, pixel positions of rim and base, crop, size, camera and groove positions. Remove all milk, coffee, foam and bubbles. Transparent clear optical glass, preserving thickness and highlights. Same 1024 × 1536 canvas, transparent background, no halo, floor, props, steam, or new artwork.

Milk edit: Keep exact glass silhouette, rim/base positions, groove shapes, size, camera and canvas. Change only the liquid to creamy natural white milk at the same fill level and meniscus, safely below the rim. Remove brown coffee/foam; retain photographic texture and refracting grooves. Transparent alpha, neutral warm light, no floor/background/glow/shadows/stream/splashes. Do not move, resize or reframe the glass.

Elevated view: Only the reference's traditional cutting-chai glass and marbled contents. Camera approximately 22 degrees above the rim, looking into the calm coffee surface. Same tapered fluted glass and thick clear base, centered full glass on portrait 2:3 canvas. Transparent alpha, neutral warm daylight, no floor/background/steam/shadow/props/text, no overflow.

These are illustrative serving visuals, not documentary farm photography or evidence of product composition or health benefits.
