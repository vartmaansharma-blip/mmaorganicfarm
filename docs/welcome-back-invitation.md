# M’ma awaits you

Database retirement is applied. Read-only verification confirms issuance is removed, both redemption entry points are denied, and no coupons or discounted orders exist. The retirement changes only executable paths; it performs no order, payment or customer data writes.

Customers cancel immediately from My M’ma → Deliveries. Unfulfilled scheduled visits and unbound drafts stop; completed delivery evidence, purchased terms and payment history remain recorded. The delivery-first account redesign and its navigation are retained.
